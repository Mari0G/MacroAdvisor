# Changelog

## [0.1.0] - 2026-09-23

First stable release.

- Track meals and drinks with editable AI nutrition estimates, goals, and local
  nutrition history.
- Capture meal photos for analysis and optionally retain small copies on device
  with user controls for removal and retention.
- Choose among four dark Type C palettes; the choice persists across restarts.
- Use the app in English or German.
- Improve nutrition completeness validation and prevent incomplete goal progress
  from overflowing.
